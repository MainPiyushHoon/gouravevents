# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Affluent couples and families planning high-end luxury and destination weddings in premier Indian destinations (Jaipur, Udaipur, Jim Corbett, Rishikesh). They are discerning, value heritage and scenic grandeur, and seek an elite wedding planner who offers genuine craftsmanship and personal founder-level attention rather than impersonal agency bureaucracy.

## Product Purpose

Establish a digital presence communicating luxury, royalty, elegance, grandeur, exclusivity, and deep emotional resonance. The site showcases the firm's wedding portfolio, establishes immediate credibility and trust, and inspires prospective clients to initiate a private consultation directly with the founder.

## Positioning

Founder-led luxury wedding curation specializing in royal heritage palaces (Jaipur, Udaipur) and evocative natural destinations (Jim Corbett, Rishikesh). Gourav personally oversees every celebration with bespoke spatial scenography, flawless hospitality, and master-level execution.

## Operating Context

Prospective clients and family decision-makers reviewing potential planners across mobile and desktop devices. They evaluate visual aesthetic, destination familiarity, breadth of services, and emotional resonance before starting a conversation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Core services: Full-scope luxury wedding planning, destination management, bespoke spatial decor & scenography, royal hospitality & logistics, artist & entertainment curation.
- Primary destinations: Jaipur, Udaipur, Jim Corbett, Rishikesh.
- Primary conversion path: Direct VIP WhatsApp connection where the founder personally handles each inquiry.
- Professional wedding photography collection is being prepared for later addition; launch experience requires high-fidelity editorial visual assets representing their destinations and design caliber.
- Strictly NOT a vendor directory or marketplace.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Gourav Events (gouravevents.com)
- Voice & Tone: Editorial, evocative, sophisticated, regal, warm, and confident ("These people create extraordinary weddings").
- Palette Direction: Obsidian (#0B0909), Deep Burgundy (#2A0E16), Rose Gold (#B76E79 as a restrained accent), Champagne (#E8C7A8), Warm Ivory (#F5EFE7), Muted Taupe (#9C8D88).
- Explicit Anti-Patterns: No bright pink, no excessive metallic gradients, no ubiquitous gold, no generic luxury clichés, no excessive ornamental clutter, and no generic template layouts.

## Product Principles

1. **Founder-Led Intimacy:** Treat every wedding as a high-touch private commission with direct founder oversight.
2. **Atmospheric Grandeur:** Honor the architectural weight of royal palaces and the organic majesty of sacred rivers and forests.
3. **Cinematic Storytelling:** Present celebrations as profound emotional narratives, not itemized event packages.
4. **Restrained Luxury:** Command elegance through typography, proportion, deep shadows, and subtle warm accents rather than ostentatious decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Ensure high-contrast readability against dark luxury backgrounds, intuitive touch-friendly navigation, smooth motion with `prefers-reduced-motion` compliance, and seamless mobile accessibility.
